DuetPartner Support

DuetPartner plays the other part of a piano duet so you can practice your own part.

Contact

Questions, problems or suggestions? Email:

treblehour@gmail.com

When you write, please include

  • Your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air)
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The name of the piece and which part you were playing, if the problem is with a particular piece
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen

Common questions

I don't hear any sound

Check that your device's volume is turned up, and that the silent switch (or Silent Mode) is off. Bluetooth headphones or speakers that are connected but turned off can also take the sound.

The playback doesn't match the score

Please email the piece name and the measure number where it goes wrong. Repeats, D.C. al Fine and other markings are supported, and examples help fix any that play incorrectly.

Can you add a piece?

Yes, suggestions are welcome. Email the title and composer of the duet you'd like to practice.
