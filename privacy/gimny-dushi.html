Гимны Души Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Гимны Души is an app by Inna Kuropatko that plays hymns with piano accompaniment and highlights the lyrics and notes. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

Гимны Души does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal details.

How the app works

Everything the app does happens on your device. Music, scores and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email treblehour@gmail.com.
